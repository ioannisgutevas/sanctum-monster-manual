Saint's Remnant. The Saint's Residue has advantage on saving throws against being turned and on checks and saving throws made to resist effects that would destroy undead.

Aura of Corrupted Faith. A 20-foot aura surrounds the Saint's Residue. Undead within this aura have advantage on attack rolls and saving throws. In addition, when a creature dies within this aura, it must succeed on a DC 16 Wisdom saving throw or rise as a Specter under the Saint's Residue's control at the start of its next turn.

Unholy Fortitude. If damage would reduce the Saint's Residue to 0 hit points, it is reduced to 1 hit point instead. Once this trait has been used, it can't be used again until the Saint's Residue finishes a long rest.

Actions

Multiattack. The Saint's Residue makes two attacks: one with its flail and one with its spectral grasp.

Flail of Penance. Melee Weapon Attack: +9 to hit, reach 10 ft., one target. Hit: 15 (2d8 + 4) bludgeoning damage plus 10 (3d6) necrotic damage.

Spectral Grasp. Melee Spell Attack: +7 to hit, reach 15 ft., one creature. Hit: 18 (4d6 + 4) necrotic damage, and the target must succeed on a DC 16 Strength saving throw or be pulled up to 10 feet toward the Saint's Residue and be restrained (escape DC 16).

Drain Devotion (Recharge 5 - 6). The Saint's Residue targets one creature it can see within 30 feet. The target must succeed on a DC 16 Wisdom saving throw or take 27 (6d8) necrotic damage, and the Saint's Residue regains a number of hit points equal to the damage dealt.

Legendary Actions

The Saint's Residue can take 3 legendary actions, choosing from the options below. Only one legendary action option can be used at a time and only at the end of another creature's turn. The Saint's Residue regains spent legendary actions at the start of its turn.

Move. The Saint's Residue moves up to half its speed.

Withering Glare (Costs 2 Actions). One creature the Saint's Residue can see within 30 feet must succeed on a DC 16 Wisdom saving throw or have disadvantage on attack rolls, ability checks, and saving throws until the end of its next turn.

Condemn (Costs 3 Actions). The Saint's Residue utters a word of blasphemous condemnation. Each creature of its choice within 30 feet that can hear it must make a DC 16 Wisdom saving throw. On a failed save, a creature takes 18 (4d8) necrotic damage and is frightened of the Saint's Residue until the end of its next turn. On a successful save, a creature takes half as much damage and isn't frightened.

Once a beacon of mercy, now a relic of betrayal. The Saint's Residue does not preach,  it punishes. It does not forgive.. only collects.

Lore

The Saint's Residue

Large Undead (Aberration), Lawful Evil

Origin

In the age before memory fractured, there was a saint whose name has been intentionally erased. She was not one of miracles, but of endurance,  chosen to bear the sins of a city that had long since forgotten devotion.

Each day, the faithful brought their confessions, their shame, their transgressions. She did not absolve them with words, but took them into herself. Blasphemy, kingslaying, betrayal, desecration.. she accepted all.

In time, the weight became unbearable. Her body broke, but she did not die. Instead, something hollow remained. Her flesh sloughed away, her mind splintered, and what rose from that ruin was not a martyr, but a vessel.

The Saint's Residue is what is left when innocence is not enough to bear evil.

Nature

The Saint's Residue roams not out of hunger, but out of purpose. It is bound to the task that defined its unlife: to accept corruption so others do not have to. It does not seek to kill, but to take in. It moves toward the guilty, the wicked, the oathbreakers.. creatures whose actions fester and stain the world.

It does not speak, for language would profane the burden it carries. Instead, it offers a silent promise: your sin can be mine. And those who accept that promise rarely leave unchanged.

Appearance

The Saint's Residue is a towering figure of rot and reverence. What remains of its face is a desiccated maw, forever open in a soundless cry. A shattered halo of rusted iron crowns its head, each spike a needle of penitence.

Tattered vestments cling to its frame, once white, now stained in layers of dried blood, ichor, and ash. Chains of iron and bone hang from its limbs, each link a reminder of a sin taken in.

Bells, censers, and relics sway from its form, chiming softly when it moves. The air around it is heavy with incense turned sour, and the faint sound of weeping that no one else can hear.

Place in the World

The Saint's Residue is both a judgment and a mercy. Clerics speak of it in hushed tones, for it represents the dangerous side of absolution..  the cost of taking on what should be repented, not transferred.

Cultists seek it out to offload their darkest deeds. Villains fear it as a cosmic debt collector. And some who walk the line between light and shadow seek it as the only being that might understand.

Hooks for Adventurers

  • The Unburdened Sin: A warlord has begun using the Saint's Residue to cleanse himself of atrocities. The party must decide whether to stop him, or take the burden for themselves.
  • Aura of Mercy: A plague of corruption spreads across the land. The only cure may lie in the Saint's Residue, but doing so requires sending someone willing or not to bear the city's sins.
  • The Silent Promise: A companion has made a secret bargain with the Saint's Residue and begins to change. Can they be saved before their soul is claimed?
