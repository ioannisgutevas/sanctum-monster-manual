Traits

Bound Territory. The hound is bound to guard a 60-foot-radius area. It cannot willingly leave this area and has advantage on attack rolls and saving throws while within it. If forcibly moved outside the boundary, it must use all its movement each turn to return.

Actions

Multiattack. The hound makes two Bite attacks.

Bite. Melee Weapon Attack: +5 to hit, reach 5 ft., one target. Hit: 7 (1d8 + 3) piercing damage plus 2 (1d4) fire damage.

Howl of the Bound (Recharge 5-6). Each creature within 30 feet that can hear it must succeed on a DC 12 Wisdom saving throw or become frightened of the hound until the end of its next turn. Creatures within the hound's bound territory have disadvantage on this saving throw.

Reactions

Guardian Lunge. When a creature the hound can see crosses into its bound territory, the hound can immediately move up to half its speed toward that creature and make one Bite attack against it.

The hound does not guard treasure. It guards a sentence written by someone who never intended to return.

The Bound That Still Hunts

Fragments from a dead estate and a loyal beast

lore

A fiendish beast bound by infernal contract to guard a specific location until its master returns. It does not understand that its master has been dead for three hundred years.

The Wrathbound Hound was once a mortal hunting dog that served a devil-touched noble. When the noble made his final pact, he offered his most loyal hound as part of the price. The transformation burned away everything except the drive to guard.

The hound patrols a specific boundary, circling endlessly around the same territory. It attacks anything that crosses the invisible threshold, fighting with mechanical precision. When nothing threatens the boundary, it sits motionless for hours, waiting.

The hound does not remember its name or the hand that once fed it. It does not know that the boundary it guards protects nothing but empty ruins. It cannot comprehend that loyalty without purpose is just another form of imprisonment.

Weakness

The hound is bound by a physical anchor within its territory - a stone marker, a buried collar, or scorched ground where the contract was sealed. Destroying this anchor releases the hound from its compulsion.

Lore Fragments

  • The hound patrols in the exact same pattern every time, following an invisible circuit.
  • Somewhere in the territory lies a stone marker, a buried collar, or a circle of scorched earth where the contract was sealed.
  • Local dogs refuse to enter the area. Birds will not roost within the territory. Plants grow but seem colorless and drained.
  • The hound leaves tracks that burn faintly for several minutes after it passes.

Encounter Hooks

  • A merchant hires the party to retrieve a lockbox from the ruins of the Marlowe estate. Inside is a contract selling the Marlowe bloodline to a devil.
  • A dying grandmother screams about her dog waiting for her. She witnessed the binding decades ago and will not die peacefully until the hound is freed.
  • Hunters find a circle of scorched ground where nothing will grow. At its center sits a stone carved with infernal script and a name: Keeper.

Lair & Scene

The hound's territory is a rough circle centered on what was once a grand estate, now collapsed to rubble and tangled vines. The air within the boundary tastes of smoke even on clear days. Three stone markers stand at equidistant points along the perimeter, each carved with a single infernal glyph that means property. At the center lies a fourth marker, larger than the others, where the grass has never grown back.

Tactics

The hound uses Guardian Lunge the instant something crosses into its territory, prioritizing creatures closest to the center of the boundary. If outnumbered, it focuses attacks on whoever moves deepest into the territory, ignoring tactical sense in favor of pure boundary enforcement.
